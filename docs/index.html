Model, data & limitations

The initial model estimates each team's scoring and points allowed from completed FBS games, shrunk toward 28 points with five prior games. Prior seasons decay by 65%. Home advantage is three points in the margin; neutral sites remove it. The advanced metrics below are comparisons, not inputs to this first model.

Historical predictions use earlier dates only. Probability scale is chosen on the calibration season and frozen for the later holdout season. Tied final scores are excluded from validation. Scores are expected values rounded to whole points; a tied projection is possible.

Passing requires at least 200 calibration and holdout games, lower Brier score than both 0.25 and a calibration-season home-win-rate baseline, lower per-team score error than a fixed 28-point baseline, and calibration error no higher than 10 percentage points. Passing is an initial held-out check, not a guarantee for future games.

Daily cached data is not a live scoreboard. Injuries, weather, lineups, betting markets and opponent adjustments are not included. Missing advanced stats remain blank. Source revisions and postponed games can delay coverage.
